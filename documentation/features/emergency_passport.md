# Offline Emergency Medical Passport

The Emergency Medical Passport provides zero-network first-responder access to critical patient health data via signed QR tokens and offline cached local storage.

---

## 🚑 Critical Data Payload
- Blood group & Rh factor
- Drug allergies (e.g., Penicillin, NSAIDs)
- Current vital medications & dosages
- Emergency guardian contacts with direct telephonic dialing links
- Organ donor status & advance medical directives
