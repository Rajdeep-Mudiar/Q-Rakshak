## 1. Introduction: Why Q-Rakshak Emergency Triage is Essential

In critical medical emergencies, **every second counts**. In India alone, over 150,000 lives are lost each year to road traffic accidents, acute cardiac episodes, and severe trauma—often exacerbated by the **Golden Hour delay**: the lack of immediate, accurate clinical context when first responders arrive on scene.

When a patient is unconscious, disoriented, or in shock:
- Responders do not know their **blood group**.
- Paramedics risk administering fatal medications due to **unknown drug allergies** (e.g., Penicillin, NSAIDs).
- Responders cannot quickly locate or notify **next-of-kin emergency contacts**.
- Crucial chronic comorbidities (Diabetes, Hypertension, Epilepsy, Hemophilia) remain undisclosed.

**Q-Rakshak** solves this problem through an instant, deterministic, zero-friction **Emergency Triage Passport and Dynamic Medical ID Card System**. Without requiring app installations or complex logins on the responder's phone, a simple physical or digital scan gives first responders immediate access to cryptographically verified, lifesaving clinical telemetry.

## 4. Hardware Assist: Shake-to-Call SOS Protocol

```
[VISUAL: Screen split showing user shaking phone; instantaneous haptic feedback & SOS dialer modal popping up.]
```

What if the patient is alone, trapped in a car, or suffering sudden chest pain and unable to navigate complex touch menus?

- **Zero-Lag Motion Sensor Hook**: Built directly into the Q-Rakshak mobile interface utilizing native `DeviceMotionEvent`.
- **Advanced High-Pass Multi-Axis Oscillation Filter**: Rejects normal car vibrations, pocket movement, and hand tremors. It triggers **only** on deliberate, vigorous shaking ($\ge 2$ directional reversals exceeding $16.5\text{ m/s}^2$).
- **Anti-Spam State Gate**: Pauses sensor triggers while the emergency prompt is open so calls are never repeatedly spammed.
- **WORM Ledger Audit**: Every trigger, dial, or dismissal is cryptographically sealed in the hospital's immutable audit log.
- **One-Tap Emergency Cellular Dialing**: Provides immediate dial buttons for designated next-of-kin and the National Ambulance Service (108).

## 5. User Step-by-Step Guide: How to Create Your Own Medical Card

Creating your personal Q-Rakshak Emergency Card and Triage Passport takes less than 2 minutes.

```
[VISUAL: Screen walkthrough recording of Q-Rakshak web dashboard.]
```

### **Step 1: Complete Your Clinical Profile & Security Settings**
1. Log into your **Q-Rakshak Dashboard**.
2. Click on **Profile & Settings** in the navigation bar.
3. Complete your clinical identity details:
   - **Personal Info**: Full Legal Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Blood Group.
   - **Emergency Contacts**: Designate your primary and secondary contacts with verified phone numbers and relationship tags.
   - **Allergies & Contraindications**: Document drug, food, or environmental sensitivities with severity indicators.
   - **Active Medications**: List current prescriptions, dosage, and frequency.
   - **National Health ID**: Link your ABHA ID and primary hospital affiliation.
4. Click **Save & Synchronize**. Your clinical passport is instantly sealed into the backend database.

---

### **Step 2: Generate, Customize, and Print Your Physical Card**
1. Navigate to the **Emergency Card** tab (or `#triage`).
2. Explore your interactive **3D Medical ID Card**:
   - Flip between **Front Face** (Identity, Blood Group, QR code) and **Back Face** (Allergies, Medications, Next of Kin, Organ Donor status).
   - Choose your card theme: **Day White** or **Matte Black**.
3. Click **Print / Save PDF**:
   - Select your layout: **Dual-Sided (Front + Back)** or individual faces.
   - Uses international **ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 standard wallet card dimensions** ($85.60 \times 53.98\text{ mm}$).
   - Click **Print Now / Save as PDF** or click **Email Me Pass** to receive a high-resolution printable copy in your inbox.
4. Cut, laminate, and keep the card in your wallet, purse, or on a lanyard.

---

### **Step 3: Instant Lifesaving Access via QR Code**
1. Anyone scanning the card's QR code using any iPhone or Android camera is immediately routed to your **Q-Rakshak Triage Passport**.
2. Zero login required for first responders to view lifesaving clinical details.
3. Responders can immediately dial your emergency contacts or hospital emergency desk with a single tap.
